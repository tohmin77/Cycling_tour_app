Apps for South Korea

Public transport

  • KakaoMap / Naver Map
    Bus, subway and train routing with live arrivals (Google Maps transit is limited in Korea)
  • KORAIL Talk
    Book KTX / ITX / Mugunghwa trains, e.g. Busan to Gyeongju
  • Kakao T
    Taxi hailing; supports foreign cards for registered users
  • T-money card
    Rechargeable transit card for buses and subways, also usable at convenience stores

Navigation

  • Naver Map
    Most accurate maps in Korea, English UI, bicycle-path layer
  • KakaoMap
    Alternative with bike route option
  • Komoot / Ride with GPS / Strava
    Cycling route guidance and recording; download GPX/offline maps in advance
  • Papago
    Naver's translator for menus and signs

Payment

  • Visa / Mastercard credit card
    Accepted almost everywhere; contactless works at most shops
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay
    Growing acceptance, but not at every merchant; carry a physical card
  • Kakao Pay / Naver Pay
    Common locally; foreign-card linking is limited, so do not rely on it
  • Cash (KRW) + T-money
    Needed for some markets and rural stalls; withdraw at convenience-store ATMs (GS25/CU/7-Eleven)
  • Wise
    Low-fee card and currency exchange for travellers
